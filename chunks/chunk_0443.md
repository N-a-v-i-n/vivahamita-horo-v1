**Example 50:** Let us say someone was born at 5:50 am on 2000 April 28 (time zone: 

4 hours west of GMT). Moon is at 2°23' in Aq at the time of birth. 

- (1) This is in the 3<sup>rd</sup> pada (quarter) of Dhanishtha constellation. Dhanishtha constellation starts at 23°20' in Cp and ends at 6°40' in Aq. 

(2) Moon’s advancement in Dhanishtha = 2Aq23 – 23Cp20 = 32°23' – 23°20' = 9°3'. 

- (3) The total length of the constellation is 13°20'. The length yet to be traversed in this constellation as a fraction of the total constellation length = (13°20' – 9°3')/13°20' = 4°17'/13°20' = (4x60+17)/(13x60+20) = 257/800 = 0.32125. This is the fraction of the constellation yet to be traversed by Moon. 

(4) First dasa belongs to the lord of Dhanishtha. It is Mars. 

- (5) Let us start from Mars. From Table 38, dasa sequence is: Mars (7 years), Rahu (18 years), Jupiter (16 years), Saturn (19 years), Mercury (17 years), Ketu (7 years), Venus (20 years), Sun (6 years), Moon (10 years).
