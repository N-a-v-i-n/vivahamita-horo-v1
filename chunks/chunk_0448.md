In the same example, the 5<sup>th</sup> star from Moon’s star is Revathi owned by Mercury. Using the 5<sup>th</sup> star to start Vimsottari dasa, we get the dasa sequence as — Mercury, Ketu, Venus, Sun _etc_ . Mercury dasa left at birth is 17 x 0.32125 = 5.46125 years. Then Ketu dasa is for 7 years, Venus dasa is for 20 years and so on. 

In the same example, the 8<sup>th</sup> star from Moon’s star is Krittika owned by Sun. Using the 8<sup>th</sup> star to start Vimsottari dasa, we get the dasa sequence as — Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu _etc_ . Sun dasa left at birth is 6 x 0.32125 = 1.9275 years. Then Moon dasa is for 10 years, Mars dasa is for 7 years and so on.
