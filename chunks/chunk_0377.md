#### **14.2 Marakas (Killers)** 

Each chart has some rasis and planets that are called marakas (killers). Since death is an event relating to the physical existence, rasi chart is of utmost importance in seeing death. Rudramsa (D-11) shows the forces of death and destruction and it can also give insight into death. D-30 shows one’s evils and punishment for the evils. Death can be a punishment for one’s evils and so we should look at D-30 also. However, the most important chart is the rasi chart. 

**The 3**<sup>**rd**</sup> **and 8**<sup>**th**</sup> **houses are the houses of life.** The 3<sup>rd</sup> house shows the vitality of one’s existence and the 8<sup>th</sup> house shows the longevity. The 12<sup>th</sup> house from any house shows losses related to the matters signified by that house. So the 12<sup>th</sup> house from these two houses shows death. **So the 2**<sup>**nd**</sup> **and 7**<sup>**th**</sup> **houses are the houses of death.** For good longevity, the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 8<sup>th</sup> houses and their lords should be strong and the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> houses and their lords should be weak.
