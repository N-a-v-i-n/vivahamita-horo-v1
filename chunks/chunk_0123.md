Part 1: Chart Analysis 

**Example 15:** Let us say Mercury is at 10° in Ge and Jupiter is at 19° in Sc. We see that 10° is in the 3<sup>rd</sup> part of the rasi and 19° is in the 6<sup>th</sup> part of the rasi. Because Ge is a dual rasi, counting starts from Le. The 3<sup>rd</sup> from Le is Li. So the 3<sup>rd</sup> part in Ge goes into Li in D-8. On the other hand, Sc is a fixed sign and counting starts from Sg. The 6<sup>th</sup> from Sg is Ta. So the 6<sup>th</sup> part of Sc goes into Ta in D-8. So Mercury is in Le and Jupiter is in Ta in D-8 for this example. 

###### **6.2.9** **_Navamsa Chart (D-9)_** 

Each rasi is divided into 9 equal parts of 3° 20' each. Bodies in the 9 parts of a rasi go into the 9 rasis starting from Ar, Cp, Li or Cn, based on whether the rasi is a fiery, earthy, airy or watery sign. 

This chart is also known as **Dharmamsa** . It is the most popular chart after rasi chart and some astrologers simply refer to it as “Amsa” (division).
