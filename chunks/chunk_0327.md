Let us continue with the rasi chart in Chart 6. From the answer to Exercise 19, we see that the BAVs of Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn have 5, 3, 4, 7, 4, 8 and 3 rekhas in Ar (respectively). Adding them all, we get 34. So SAV has 34 rekhas in Ar and we write 34 in Ar. We can find the number of rekhas in all the rasis in the same manner and prepare a chart with the number of rekhas written in each rasi.
