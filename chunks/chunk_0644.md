Thus we should look at the rasis occupied and aspected by planets in their transit and find out which houses and planets occupy those rasis in the natal chart. Based on that, we should guess the results given by the planets. **A planet occupying or aspecting a rasi in transit** **_influences_ the matters signified by the houses and planets stationed in that rasi in the natal chart.** The exact nature of the influence exerted by a planet depends on its inherent nature and the matters it stands for in the natal chart. For example, if the 5<sup>th</sup> lord in the natal chart is occupying the 8<sup>th</sup> house from natal lagna in transit, some troubles related to children may be expected. If Mars is the 8<sup>th</sup> lord in the natal chart and aspects, in transit, the rasis occupied by Venus and 7<sup>th</sup> lord in the natal chart, then some disturbances in marital life may be expected.
