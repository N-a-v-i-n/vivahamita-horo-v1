#### **26.7 Latta (Kick)** 

Latta is a nakshatra-based planetary kick. Each planet has latta (kick) on a constellation based on its transit position. If a transit planet has latta on the constellation occupied by Moon (or lagna) in natal chart, then we may expect some unfavorable results related to the signification of the planet in natal chart. 

###### **Purolatta (forward kick):** 

- (1) Sun has latta on the 12<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the forward direction. If Sun is in Mrigasira, for example, he has latta on the 12<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from Mrigasira, _i.e._ Visakha.

- (2) Mars has latta on the 3<sup>rd</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the forward direction. If Mars is in Mrigasira, for example, he has latta on the 3<sup>rd</sup> nakshatra from Mrigasira, _i.e._ Punarvasu.
