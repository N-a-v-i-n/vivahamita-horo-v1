###### **12.7.3** **_Sodhya Pindas_** 

After carrying out Trikona sodhana and then Ekaadhipatya sodhana on the BAV of a planet, we get the “Sodhita Ashtakavarga” of that planet. We denote Sodhita Ashtakavarga with SoAV.<sup>45</sup> From it, we find “Sodhya Pinda” of each planet. This will be used in transit analysis. 

For each rasi, we multiply the number in that rasi in SoAV with the multiplier of that rasi (shown in Table 28). We find such a product for all the 12 rasis. We find the sum of the products of all the 12 rasis and the sum is called rasi pinda. 

For each planet, we multiply the number in the rasi containing that planet with the multiplier of that planet (shown in Table 29). We find such a product for all the 7 planets. We find the sum of the products of all the 7 planets and the sum is called graha pinda.
