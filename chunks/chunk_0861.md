For example, we saw in Example 58 that Mercury (8<sup>th</sup> lord in 7<sup>th</sup> ) and Rahu (malefic in 2<sup>nd</sup> ) were the strongest killer planets. By taking a variation of Vimsottari dasa that starts from the 8<sup>th</sup> star from Moon’s star, we found that the native passed away in Rahu’s antardasa in Mercury’s dasa. However, one relying on standard Vimsottari dasa always will find that Venus dasa is running and he will explain the event based on the situation of Venus in the 7<sup>th</sup> house. But, if we take yogakaraka Venus to be a maraka because he is in the 7<sup>th</sup> house, we can take 6 planets (occupants and lords of 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> ) as marakas. With 6 planets out of 9 qualifying as marakas, how can we predict when the native passes away? On the other hand, by using the most appropriate dasa, we see that the event was given by the strongest candidates.
