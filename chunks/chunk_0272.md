Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach 

**Sankha Yoga:** If (1) lagna lord is strong and (2) 5<sup>th</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> lords are in mutual quadrants, then this yoga is present. Alternately, this yoga is present if (1) lagna lord and 10<sup>th</sup> lord are together in a movable sign and (2) the 9<sup>th</sup> lord is strong. One born with this yoga is blessed with wealth, spouse and children. He is kind, pious, intelligent and long-lived. Sankha means a conch shell. 

**Bheri Yoga:** If (1) the 9<sup>th</sup> lord is strong and (2) 1<sup>st</sup> , 2<sup>nd</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses are occupied by planets, then this yoga is present. Alternately, this is yoga is present if (1) the 9<sup>th</sup> lord is strong and (2) Jupiter, Venus and lagna lord are in mutual quadrants. One born with this yoga is blessed with wealth, spouse and children. He can be a king. He has fame and character. He is virtuous and religious. He enjoys pleasures. Bheri means a kettledrum.
