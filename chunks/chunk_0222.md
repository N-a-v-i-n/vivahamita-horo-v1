#### **10.2 Graha Drishri** 

All planets aspect the 7<sup>th</sup> house from them. For example, Sun in Ta aspects Sc. Mars in Ge aspects Sg. Moon in Le aspects Aq. Jupiter in Pi aspects Vi. Saturn in Cp aspects Cn. Find the 7<sup>th</sup> house from the planet and the planet aspects that house. 

In addition, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn have special aspects: 

- Jupiter aspects the 5<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> houses from him, in addition to the 7<sup>th</sup> house. 

- Mars aspects the 4<sup>th</sup> and 8<sup>th</sup> houses from him, in addition to the 7<sup>th</sup> house. 

- Saturn aspects the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> houses from him, in addition to the 7<sup>th</sup> house. 

We can decide the signs and houses aspected by a planet as above. If any planet occupies the aspected houses, then the planet is also aspected. For example, Jupiter in Ta will aspect Saturn in Cp, because Cp is the 9<sup>th</sup> house from Ta and Jupiter aspects the 9<sup>th</sup> from him.
