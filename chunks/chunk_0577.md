#### **23.5 Death of Near Relatives** 

Shoola dasa shows Shiva’s punishment. It shows misfortune, suffering and death. Using it, death of near relatives can also be timed. 

For each relative, we treat the house that shows him/her as lagna and find Shoola dasa based on it. Shoola dasa starts from the stronger of that rasi and the 7<sup>th</sup> from it. We have different names for Shoola dasas started from different houses. For example, Pitri Shoola dasa (pitri = father) starts from the stronger of 9<sup>th</sup> and 3<sup>rd</sup> houses. It is used in the timing of father’s death. Bhratri Shoola dasa (bhratri = brother) also starts from the stronger of 3<sup>rd</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> houses and it shows the death of younger siblings. Matri Shoola dasa (matri = mother) starts from the stronger of 4<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> houses. Dara Shoola dasa (dara = wife) starts from the stronger of 7<sup>th</sup> and 1<sup>st</sup> houses and it shows the death of wife (this dasa will be identical to the native’s normal Shoola dasa). Putra Shoola dasa (putra = son) starts from the stronger of 5<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses and it shows the death of a child.
