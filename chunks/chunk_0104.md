#### **5.2 Bhaava Lagna** 

Bhaava lagna is at the position of Sun at the time of sunrise. It moves at the rate of **one rasi per 2 hours** . In the rest of this book, bhava lagna will be denoted by **BL** .<sup>10</sup> 

If sunrise takes place at 6:00 am and Sun is at 6s 4°47' then, horalagna is at 6s 4°47' at 6:00 am, at 6s 19°47' at 7:00 am, at 7s 4°47' at 8:00 am, 8s 4°47' at 10:00 am and so on. Bhavalagna moves at the rate of 1° per 4 minutes ( _i.e._ , 15° per hour).
