There are other maraka grahas too. If a malefic planet powerfully conjoins or aspects, using graha drishti, the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> houses or their lords, then it qualifies as a maraka graha. Let us look at an example. Suppose lagna is in Le, Saturn is in Sg and Mars is in Ge. Then Saturn is a maraka on account of owning the 7<sup>th</sup> house (Aq). Mars is a malefic and he is in Ge. He aspects the 2<sup>nd</sup> house (Vi, with the 4<sup>th</sup> house aspect) and the 7<sup>th</sup> lord (Saturn in Sg – with the 7<sup>th</sup> house aspect). Because of these two factors, Mars is a maraka. Let us take another example. Suppose lagna is in Pi, Mars is in Ge, Mercury is in Cp and Saturn is in Ar. On account of owning the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> houses, Mars and Mercury are marakas. Look at Saturn. He is in the 2<sup>nd</sup> house and aspects the 2<sup>nd</sup> lord Mars (with the 3<sup>rd</sup> house aspect) and the 7<sup>th</sup> lord Mercury (with the 10<sup>th</sup> house aspect). So Saturn is also a maraka and he may in fact be a stronger maraka than Mars and Mercury.
