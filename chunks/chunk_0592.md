Let us go through the procedure used in finding dasas and antardasas, in case it is not yet obvious to the reader: 

- (1) Find the nakshatra pada occupied by natal Moon. Identify the 9-rasi sequence associated with it, using Table 44-Table 47. Also, note down the paramayush.

- (2) Find the fraction of the nakshatra pada that was covered by Moon at birth. 

- (3) Find the same fraction of the paramayush. That represents the portion of the paramayush (dasas of the nine rasis) that was over before birth. Based on this, we can find which of the nine dasas runs at birth and how much of the dasa remains at birth.
