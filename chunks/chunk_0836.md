Which dasa should we use for financial matters? It is Sudasa. Let us find Sudasa. 

Moon is at 13º13'25'' in Leo. He is in Makha constellation, which runs from 0º to 13º20' in Le. His advancement in nakshatra is (13º13'25'')/ (13º20') = (793.4167')/(800') = 0.99177. The same fraction of the entire zodiac is 0.99177x360 = 357.0375º = 357º 2' 15''. Adding this to lagna, we get Sree Lagna. Lagna is at 17º35'50'' in Pi. So we get Sree Lagna at 14º38'5'' in Pi. Sudasa starts from Pi. Because Sree Lagna is in an even rasi, Sudasa goes backward. We reckon quadrants, panapharas and apoklimas backwards and get Pi, Sg, Vi, Ge, Aq, Sc, Le, Ta, Cp, Li, Cn and Ar. Dasa periods are as in Narayana dasa. However, there is an exception for the first dasa. Because Jupiter is in Pi, Pi dasa is for 12 years. However, only a fraction of this is left at birth, based on the fraction of the rasi yet to be traversed by Sree Lagna. This fraction is (30º – 14º38'5'')/ 30º = 0.51218. So the remainder of Pi dasa at birth is 0.51218x12 years = 6 years 1 month 23 days.
