## **29. Tajaka Yogas** 

#### **29.1 Introduction** 

Just as we have yogas in natal charts, we have special combinations with special results that are applicable to Tajaka annual/monthly charts. These are called Tajaka yogas. These yogas are applicable to Tajaka annual charts as well as prasna charts (horary charts). We will briefly go over their definitions in this chapter. 

#### **29.2 Definitions** 

###### **29.2.1** **_Ishkavala Yoga_** 

If planets occupy only kendras (1<sup>st</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> houses) and panapharas (2<sup>nd</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses) and if apoklimas (3<sup>rd</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> , 9<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses) are empty, then this yoga is present. This yoga gives wealth, happiness and good fortune.
