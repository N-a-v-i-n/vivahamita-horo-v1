Let us take another example. Domestic harmony, comfort, well-being and happiness are seen from the 4<sup>th</sup> house. What are the things that can make or break domestic harmony? Of course, wife, children and other family members! Each of them can intervene in one’s domestic harmony. No wonder the 5<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 2<sup>nd</sup> houses have an argala on the 4<sup>th</sup> house (being the 2<sup>nd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> respectively from the 4<sup>th</sup> house)! If a malefic in 5<sup>th</sup> has papargala on 4<sup>th</sup> , it may show domestic clashes and lack of sukha (comfort/happiness) on account of a child. If a benefic in 7<sup>th</sup> has subhargala on on 4<sup>th</sup> , it may show domestic happiness due to a caring partner.
