If all the three pairs result in the same longevity category, that will be the combined classification. If two pairs give one result and the third pair gives a different result, then the result given by two pairs dominates. Parasara gave further hints regarding this case and suggested finding the maximum longevity (paramaayush) of a person using Table 34.
