Each rasi is divided into 6 equal parts of 5° each. Bodies in the 6 parts of a rasi go into the 6 rasis starting from Ar or Li, based on whether the rasi is odd or even. 

**Example 13:** Let us say Mercury is at 11° in Ge and Jupiter is at 19° in Sc. We see that 11° is in the 3<sup>rd</sup> part of the rasi and 19° is in the 4<sup>th</sup> part of the rasi. Ge is an odd rasi and counting starts from Ar. The 3<sup>rd</sup> from Ar is Ge. So the 3<sup>rd</sup> part in Ge goes into Ge in D-6. On the other hand, Sc is an even rasi and counting starts from Li. The 4<sup>th</sup> from Li is Cp. So the 4<sup>th</sup> part of Sc goes into Cp in D-6. So Mercury is in Ge and Jupiter is in Cp in D-6 for this example. 

###### **6.2.7** **_Saptamsa Chart (D-7)_** 

Each rasi is divided into 7 equal parts of 4° 17' 8.57''. Bodies in the 1<sup>st</sup> , 2<sup>nd</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> parts of a rasi go into the 7 rasis starting from the rasi itself, if it is an odd rasi, or starting from the 7<sup>th</sup> sign from it, if it is an even rasi.
