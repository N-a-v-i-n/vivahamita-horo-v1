###### **29.2.5** **_Nakta Yoga_** 

Suppose two planets have an aspect, but there is no ithasala yoga or eesarpha yoga. Then, we say that there is Nakta yoga between the two planets, if a planet that moves faster than both the planets has an aspect with both and forms ithasala yoga with both. This shows fulfillment of the matters represented by the first 2 planets with the help of someone shows by the 3<sup>rd</sup> planet.
