Natural malefics in the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> from dasa lagna give success in ventures. Natural benefics in those houses give failures. Natural benefics in trines and 8<sup>th</sup> from dasa lagna give happiness and success. Natural malefics in those houses give failures, obstructions and unhappiness. Situation of a planet – benefic or a malefic – in the 11<sup>th</sup> house ensures gains. Rahu in the 8<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses from dasa lagna gives constant fear. If the lord of dasa lagna or the lord of a trine or a quadrant from it is exalted or in own house, it gives excellent results. Debilitation, on the other hand, is bad. If the lord of a dusthana from dasa lagna is debilitated, it gives good results. If the 4<sup>th</sup> house from dasa lagna has malefics, there will be discomfort and lack of happiness. Benefics in the same house give happiness, well-being and pleasures. Benefics in the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 5<sup>th</sup> houses from dasa lagna give good name, fame and favors from authorities.
