#### **26.5 Nakshatra-based Aspects** 

Sun and Moon aspect the 14<sup>th</sup> and 15<sup>th</sup> constellations from them. Mars aspects the 1<sup>st</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 15<sup>th</sup> constellations from him. Mercury and Venus aspect the 1<sup>st</sup> and 15<sup>th</sup> constellations from them. Jupiter aspects the 10<sup>th</sup> , 15<sup>th</sup> and 19<sup>th</sup> constellations from him. Saturn aspects the 3<sup>rd</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 15<sup>th</sup> and 19<sup>th</sup> constellations from him. 

A natural benefic gives good results related to the constellations aspected by it and a natural malefic gives bad results related to the constellations aspected by it.
