Thus an erudite astrologer should make proper modifications to the rules when a planet is retrograde or about to become retrograde. 

###### **29.2.4** **_Eesarpha Yoga_** 

Easarpha yoga is the opposite of ithasala. If two planets have an aspect and the faster moving planet has a _higher_ advancement in its rasi than the slower moving planet, then we have eesarpha yoga. 

_Results_ : This is a bad yoga. This results in failures and disappointments. If lagna lord has eesarpha yoga with 5<sup>th</sup> lord or putra saham lord or Jupiter, some disappointments related to children are possible. If lagna lord has an eesarpha with 10<sup>th</sup> lord or 5<sup>th</sup> lord or raajya saham lord or Sun in the chart of a president or prime minister or king, loss of power is a possibility during the year. 

_Example_ : Suppose Moon is at 23º in Le and Venus is at 19º in Li. They have a sextile aspect. Both the planets are within the deeptaamsa (orb) of the other. Moon’s advancement in his rasi is 23º and Venus’s advancement in his rasi is 19º. Faster planet (Moon) is more advanced than the slower planet (Venus). So we have eesarpha yoga.
