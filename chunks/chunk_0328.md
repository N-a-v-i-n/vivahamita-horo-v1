A rasi with 30 or more rekhas becomes strong. Matters signified by the house falling in such a rasi flourish and planets transiting in such a rasi bring good results. A rasi with 25-30 rekhas is average. A rasi with less than 25 rekhas becomes weak. Matters signified by the house falling in such a rasi suffer and planets transiting in such a rasi bring bad results. 

When choosing muhurtas<sup>43</sup> for auspicious activities like a wedding or housewarming, one should look at the strengths, as per SAV of the natal chart, of the rasis containing lagna, Moon and Sun in the muhurta chart. Rasis containing 30 or more rekhas in SAV are favorable.
