**Vishnu Yoga:** If (1) the 9<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> lords are in the 2<sup>nd</sup> house and (2) the lord of the sign occupied in navamsa by the 9<sup>th</sup> lord in rasi chart is also in the 2<sup>nd</sup> house, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is fortunate, learned, long-lived and liked by kings. He is a worshipper of Vishnu. 

**Siva Yoga:** If (1) the 5<sup>th</sup> lord is in the 9<sup>th</sup> house, (2) the 9<sup>th</sup> lord is in the 10<sup>th</sup> house, and, (3) the 10<sup>th</sup> lord is in the 5<sup>th</sup> house, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is wise and virtuous. He is a conqueror. He can be an army chief or a businessman. Lord Siva is one of the Trinity of Gods.
