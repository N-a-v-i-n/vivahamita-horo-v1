To see the part occupied by a body, we can take its longitude from the beginning of the occupied rasi, multiply it by 2, take degrees and ignore minutes, add 1 to it. 

**Example 26:** Let us say Jupiter is at 222° 58' , _i.e._ 12° 58' in Scorpio. Multiplying 12° 58' by 2, we get 25° 56'. Taking degrees and ignoring minutes, we get 25. Adding 1, we get 26. So 12° 58' is in the 26<sup>th</sup> part of rasi (where each part is 1/60<sup>th</sup> of rasi, _i.e._ 30'). So we have to count the 26<sup>th</sup> rasi from Sc. Removing multiples of 12 from 26, we get 2. The 2<sup>nd</sup> rasi from Sc is Sg. So Jupiter is in Sg in D-60 in this example.
