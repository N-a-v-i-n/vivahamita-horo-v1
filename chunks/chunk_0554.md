#### **22.2 Computation and Interpretation** 

###### **22.2.1** **_Computation_** 

Find the stronger of the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 8<sup>th</sup> houses. Start from the rasi containing that house. If the rasi is odd, go in the forward (zodiacal) direction and cover the 12 rasis. If the rasi is even, go in the backward (anti-zodiacal) direction and cover the 12 rasis. Dasas of movable, fixed and dual rasis have 7, 8 and 9 years respectively.<sup>60</sup> 

Classics are not clear about how we go about finding antardasas. This author suggests using the same rules used for Narayana dasa. 

###### **22.2.2** **_Interpretation_** 

Dasa of strong maraka rasis and dasas of rasis containing strong maraka grahas can bring death. Usually death occurs in the dasa of a Trishoola rasi. There are three Trishoola rasis and we can identify the correct rasi based on the longevity category (short, middle or long life).
