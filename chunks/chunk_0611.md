**Chart 48** 



Lagna in D-12 is in Vi. The 9<sup>th</sup> house is in Ta. The 9<sup>th</sup> house in D-12 shows the relation with father and the associated happiness. It stands for the “concept” of father and paternal guidance. The “physical body” of the father is an _illusion_ related to the concept of father and A9 represents it. So we can use the 9<sup>th</sup> lord or A9 to see the physical body of father. Here A9 is in Cp. Taking Cp as lagna, we see that Cn is the 7<sup>th</sup> house of death and its lord Moon is exalted. Moon is not only exalted, but he afflicts Sun, who owns the 8<sup>th</sup> house from Cp. So Cn-Cn antardasa can result in the death of father. Moreover, Cn dasa here comes after Vi and involves mandooki gati (frog’s leap). We see from the previous discussions that mandooki gati in savya nakshatras can bring distress to father.
