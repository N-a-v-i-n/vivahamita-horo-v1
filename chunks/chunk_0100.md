_Daytime births_ : The first part is ruled by the lord of weekday and then we cover planets in the order of weekdays. The part after the one ruled by Saturn is lord-less. After that, Sun’s part comes. For example, the first 1/8<sup>th</sup> of the daytime on a Thursday is ruled by Jupiter. Next part is ruled by Venus. The 3<sup>rd</sup> part is ruled by Saturn. The 4<sup>th</sup> part is lord-less. The 5<sup>th</sup> part is ruled by Sun. The 6<sup>th</sup> part is ruled by Moon. The 7<sup>th</sup> planet is ruled by Mars. The 8<sup>th</sup> part is ruled by Mercury.
