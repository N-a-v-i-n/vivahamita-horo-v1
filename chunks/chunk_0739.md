- (6) **Semi-sextile aspect:** A planet has a neutral aspect on the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses from it and on the planets in those houses. 

Deeptamsa is “the orb of an aspect”. Deeptamsas of planets: Sun – 15º, Moon – 12º, Mars – 8º, Mercury – 7º, Jupiter – 9º, Venus – 7º, Saturn – 9º. If Venus is at 13º in Li, he will have a trinal aspect on Ge, but his aspect doesn’t cover the entire rasi. Venusian aspect covers an angle of 7º from 13º in Gemini mainly, though Venus may have a moderate aspectual influence on the entire rasi. So Venus _mainly_ influences 6º–20º in Ge by aspect. Deeptamsa is the same for all kinds of  aspects.
