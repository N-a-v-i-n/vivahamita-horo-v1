Jaimini taught that the 11<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses from AL show financial gains and expenditures. Natural malefics in the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> houses from AL show someone who is perceived as a bold person who hits enemies hard. Since such impressions are usually formed about materially successful people, malefics in the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> from AL make one bold and materially successful. Natural benefics in the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> houses from AL make one very gentle and restrained in public behavior. Such a person does not fight with others boldly. This combination is usually found in the charts of saints and saintly and mild-natured people. 

The 10<sup>th</sup> house from lagna in D-10 shows one’s true conduct in society. It shows the one’s career and the true nature of one’s karma (work). The 10<sup>th</sup> house from AL in D-10 shows perceptions about one’s conduct in society. It deals more with one’s status in career.
