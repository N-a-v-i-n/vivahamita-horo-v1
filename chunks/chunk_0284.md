**Devendra Yoga:** If (1) lagna is in a fixed sign, (2) 2<sup>nd</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> lords have an exchange<sup>35</sup> , and, (3) lagna and 11<sup>th</sup> lords have an exchange, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is a leader of men. He is handsome, romantic, long-lived and famous. Devendra is the ruler of gods. 

**Indra Yoga:** If (1) the 5<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> lords have an exchange and (2) Moon occupies the 5<sup>th</sup> house, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga becomes a king. He is bold, famous and long-lived. Indra is the ruler of gods. 

**Ravi Yoga:** If (1) Sun is in the 10<sup>th</sup> house and (2) the 10<sup>th</sup> lord is in the 3<sup>rd</sup> house with Saturn, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is learned, passionate and respected by kings. Ravi means Sun.
