We can find houses from houses and concatenate the meanings in some places. For example, the 3<sup>rd</sup> house shows younger brother. The 2<sup>nd</sup> house from the 3<sup>rd</sup> house  is the 4<sup>th</sup> house (count 1, 2 from 3<sup>rd</sup> and get 3<sup>rd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> ). So the 4<sup>th</sup> house shows the wealth, speech etc of younger brother. The 7<sup>th</sup> house from the 3<sup>rd</sup> house is the 9<sup>th</sup> house and it can show younger sibling’s spouse. The 11<sup>th</sup> house from lagna shows friends and the 4<sup>th</sup> house from lagna is nothing but the 6<sup>th</sup> house from the 11<sup>th</sup> house. So the 4<sup>th</sup> house stands for enemies, diseases and debts of friends. In this manner, we can deduce many additional meanings of various houses.
