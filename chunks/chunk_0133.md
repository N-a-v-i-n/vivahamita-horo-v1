###### **6.2.18** **_Khavedamsa Chart (D-40)_** 

Each rasi is divided into 40 equal parts of 45' each. Bodies in the 40 parts of a rasi go into the 40 rasis starting from Ar or Li, based on whether the rasi is odd or even. 

This chart is also called **Chatvarimsamsa** .
