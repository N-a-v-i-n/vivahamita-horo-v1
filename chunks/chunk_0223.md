###### **Example 34:** 

Let us say Jupiter is in Ge, Mars in Le and Saturn in Sg. Jupiter in Ge will aspect the 5<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> from Ge. So Jupiter in Ge will aspect Li, Sg and Aq. Mars in Le will aspect the 4<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 8<sup>th</sup> from Le. So Mars in Le will aspect Sc, Aq and Pi. Saturn in Sg will aspect the 3<sup>rd</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> from Sg. So Saturn in Sg will aspect Aq, Ge and Vi. 

Look at a few charts and figure out which planets are aspecting which houses and which planets are aspecting which planets. With experience, you can become good at it and this is an important skill required in interpreting charts.
