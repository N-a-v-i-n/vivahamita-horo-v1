###### **15.5.2** **_Stronger Rasi_** 

When computing rasi dasas, we sometimes need to find the strongest rasi out of 2 or 3 or 4 rasis. In this book, we will mainly use Narayana dasa and we will only need to compare the strengths of 2 rasis. To conclude that a rasi is stronger than another rasi for the purpose of rasi dasas, we use the following rules. We use the same rules for finding the stronger rasi owned by a planet, when computing its graha arudha. We go from one rule to the next only if there is no winner after the rule. When we have a winner, we stop and do not go to the next rule. 

- (1) If one rasi contains more planets than the other rasi, then it is stronger. Suppose we are comparing the strengths of Ar and Li. Suppose Ar contains Saturn and Jupiter and Li contains Venus. Then Ar is stronger, as it contains more planets.
