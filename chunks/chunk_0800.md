#### **31.3 Dasa Computation** 

Dasas of the 12 houses run in cycles throughout a native’s life. Each dasa is for one year. For example, dasa of the 1<sup>st</sup> house runs in the 1<sup>st</sup> year of one’s life. Dasa of the 2<sup>nd</sup> house runs in the 2<sup>nd</sup> year of one’s life. After 12 years, dasa of the 1<sup>st</sup> house will return in the 13<sup>th</sup> year. Dasa of the 2<sup>nd</sup> house will return in the 14<sup>th</sup> year. After every 12 years, the same dasas keep coming. 

One year stands for a solar year here. A new dasa starts after every one year. The exact date and time when a new SC dasa starts can be found by casting Tajaka annual chart. Look at the number of years completed. Adding one to it, you get the year that starts. Divide it by 12 and find the remainder (if the remainder is zero, make it 12). This remainder gives the house whose dasa runs in the year. 

For example, let us take a native who finishes 44 years and starts the 45<sup>th</sup> year of his life on a particular date. Because the remainder when 45 is divided by 12 is 9, SC dasa of the 9<sup>th</sup> house runs in the 45<sup>th</sup> year.
