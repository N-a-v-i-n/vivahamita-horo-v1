In D-3, we see siblings. The 3<sup>rd</sup> house shows younger sibling and the 11<sup>th</sup> house shows elder sibling. Being the 3<sup>rd</sup> from the 3<sup>rd</sup> house, the 5<sup>th</sup> house shows younger brother’s younger brother, _i.e._ second younger brother. Being the 11<sup>th</sup> from the 11<sup>th</sup> house, the 9<sup>th</sup> house shows elder brother’s elder brother, _i.e._ second elder brother. We take the 3<sup>rd</sup> lord, 5<sup>th</sup> lord, 7<sup>th</sup> lord _etc_ as lagnas of first (immediate) **younger** sibling, second younger sibling, third younger sibling _etc_ . We take the 11<sup>th</sup> lord, 9<sup>th</sup> lord, 7<sup>th</sup> lord _etc_ as lagnas of first (immediate) **elder** sibling, second elder sibling, third elder sibling _etc_ . 

Similarly, the 5<sup>th</sup> house house shows children in D-7. The 7<sup>th</sup> house is the 3<sup>rd</sup> from 5<sup>th</sup> and shows one’s child’s younger sibling. So the 5<sup>th</sup> lord shows the first child, the 7<sup>th</sup> lord shows the second child, the 9<sup>th</sup> house shows the third child and so on.
