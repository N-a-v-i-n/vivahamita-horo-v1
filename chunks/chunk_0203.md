One important arudha pada used in Jyotish is upapada lagna (UL) – the arudha pada of the 12<sup>th</sup> house. This shows one’s marriage and spouse. Planets in UL show the kind of marriage one has and the kind of spouse one gets. For example, Mercury in UL can show intelligent (when good) or indecisive (when bad) spouse. Ketu in UL can show spiritual (when good) or short-tempered (when bad) spouse. Sun in UL can show a charming spouse from a respectable family. It can also show an authoritative spouse. Mars in UL may show a bold spouse (when good) and a quarrelsome spouse (when bad). 

The 8<sup>th</sup> house from UL shows the longevity of marriage and the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> houses from UL show the end of marriage. Malefics like Mars, Saturn and nodes in these houses from UL can result in troubles for the marriage and even a divorce.
