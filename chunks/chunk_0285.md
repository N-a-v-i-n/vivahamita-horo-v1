**Bhaaskara Yoga:** If (1) Moon is in the 12<sup>th</sup> from Sun, (2) Mercury is in the 2<sup>nd</sup> from Sun, and, (3) Jupiter is in the 5<sup>th</sup> or 9<sup>th</sup> from Moon, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is wealthy, valorous and aristocratic. He is learned in sastras, astrology and music. He has a good personality. Bhaaskara means “one with bright rays”. It is a name of Sun. 

**Kulavardhana Yoga:** If each planet occupies the 5<sup>th</sup> house from either lagna or Moon or Sun, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is happy, wealthy and brings name to his lineage and community. He has an unbroken line of worthy successors. Kula means “lineage or community”. Vardhana means “one who makes it grow and prosper”.
