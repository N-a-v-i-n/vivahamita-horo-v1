The ring must be worn for the first time on the weekday of the planet. The planetary strength should be good at that time. The planet ruling the gemstone should be strong at that time. 

We can combine two gemstones and wear them, if the corresponding planets are friends and functional benefics. This is partciularly recommended when the two planets form a yoga. Suppose someone has lagna in Virgo. If 10<sup>th</sup> lord Mercury and 9<sup>th</sup> lord Venus are together, they form dharma-karmadhipati yoga. A ring consisting of emerald and diamond may be advised. Similarly, if Sun and Moon conjoin in the chart of a person with Aries lagna, they form a raja yoga and a ring with ruby and pearl may be recommended.
