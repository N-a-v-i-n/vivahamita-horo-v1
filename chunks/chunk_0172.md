Trines from the 3<sup>rd</sup> house are called “kaama trikonas” (trines of desire) and they show one’s desires and how one gets them. The 3<sup>rd</sup> house shows one’s persistence. Trines from the 4<sup>th</sup> house (4<sup>th</sup> – harmony, 8<sup>th</sup> – occult studies and spiritual awakening and 12<sup>th</sup> – moksha) are called “moksha trikonas” (trines of liberation) and they show how one follows _moksha_ – the fourth purpose of human existence.
