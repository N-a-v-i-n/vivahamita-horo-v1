The commencement of a new year is called “varsha pravesh” by some people. Varsha means a year and pravesh means entry. 

###### **Example 118:** 

Let us take a native with this birthdata: 8<sup>th</sup> March 1967, 5:40 pm (IST), 73 E 04, 26 N 18. Sun occupies 23° 50' 25'' in Aq in his birthchart. Suppose we want analyze the one-year period of March 2000-March 2001 using Tajaka annual chart. 

Then we should find the date and time when Sun enters 23° 50' 25'' in Aq in March 2000. We find that Sun enters this position at 4:41:21 am on 8<sup>th</sup> March 2000. The native finishes 33 years and enters his 34<sup>th</sup> year at that time. 

We can erect a chart with the following data: 8<sup>th</sup> March 2000, 4:41:21 am (IST), 73 E 04, 26 N 18<sup>76</sup> . That chart is called the native’s Tajaka annual chart for 2000-2001.
