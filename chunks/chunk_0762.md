_Results_ : This is a good yoga and this shows fulfillment of the matters represented by the two planets in ithasala. Suppose we want to analyze prospects for a particular matter. Then  ithasala involving lagna lord, lord of the related house, lord of the related saham or the naisargika karaka (natural significator) will show good results related to the matter. For example, ithasala between lagna lord and 7<sup>th</sup> lord (or the lord of vivaha saham or Venus) shows that the native may get married in the year. 

_Example_ : Suppose Moon is at 14º in Le and Venus is at 19º in Li. They have a sextile aspect. Both the planets are within the deeptaamsa (orb) of the other. Moon’s advancement in his rasi is 14º and Venus’s advancement in his rasi is 19º. Faster planet (Moon) is less advanced than the slower planet (Venus). So we have ithasala yoga.
