If the 5<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> lords conjoin and aspect lagna, we learnt that it forms a rajayoga. Here 5<sup>th</sup> lord Moon and 9<sup>th</sup> lord Mars join in Vi and aspect lagna in Pi. This shows a powerful rajayoga giving a powerful and prosperous position related to religious matters. Venus, the planet of passion, is in the 8<sup>th</sup> house in own rasi. That shows sincere efforts and hard-work in religious matters. The 2<sup>nd</sup> house has Sun and Mercury in it. The 2<sup>nd</sup> house shows speech among other things. Mercury is the planet of speech and he has 6 rekhas in his D-20 BAV. Sun is a charismatic planet and he has 5 rekhas in D-20 BAV. They together give Budha-Aaditya yoga in the house of speech. The native is likely to be an excellent orator of religious matters.
