###### **29.2.2** **_Induvara Yoga_** 

If planets occupy only apoklimas (3<sup>rd</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> , 9<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses) and if kendras (1<sup>st</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> houses) and panapharas (2<sup>nd</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses) are empty, then this yoga is present. This yoga gives disappointments, worries and illnesses. 

###### **29.2.3** **_Ithasala Yoga_** 

If two planets have an aspect and if the faster moving planet<sup>83</sup> is less advanced in its rasi than the slower moving planet, then we have an ithasala yoga between the two. In western astrology, this is called an “applying aspect”. 

> 83 In the increasing order of speed, planets can be listed as: Saturn, Rahu/Ketu, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury and Moon.
