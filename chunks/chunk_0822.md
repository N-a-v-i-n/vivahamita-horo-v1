###### **32.2.2** **_Dasas_** 

Rasi dasas based on rasi chart change only if lagna changes rasi or a planet changes rasi. Because lagna changes rasi once in 2 hours, we do not have to deal with inaccuracies in rasi dasas in most cases. 

Narayana dasa of divisional charts does not change in a small period of time, unless a planet changes rasi in the divisional chart of interest.
