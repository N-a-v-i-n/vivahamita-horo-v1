( _iii_ ) _Bhavishya ithasala yoga_ is formed if vartamaana ithasala yoga _is about to_ be formed when the faster moving planet moves by 1º or less. Bhavishya means future. Bhavishya ithasala shows fulfillment after some obstructions or delay. Suppose Moon is at 13°35' in Le and Venus is at 21º20' in Le. There is a sextile aspect. Moon’s deeptaamsa is 12º and hence his sextile aspect on Li extends from 1º35' to 25º35' in Li (subtract and add 12º, Moon’s deeptaamsa, from/to 13º35'). Venus is within Moon’s deeptaamsa.
