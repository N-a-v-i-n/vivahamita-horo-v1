#### **11.9 Dhana Yogas** 

Dhana means wealth. Dhana yogas are combinations that give one abundant riches. 

**Basic Principle:** The 5<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> lords and planets joining them are capable of giving money. They give dasas in their dasas. The 11<sup>th</sup> house is also important for material gains and it should be strong. The 2<sup>nd</sup> house is also important. If Moon, Mercury, Jupiter or Venus is exalted in the 2<sup>nd</sup> house, it makes the native very rich. 

In addition to these general principles, Parasara listed specific combinations for various lagnas. In all these combinations, the strength of the participating planets decides the magnitude of results experienced. 

- (1) **For Ar lagna:** If Sun is in the 5<sup>th</sup> house and Saturn, Moon and Jupiter are in the 11<sup>th</sup> house, one becomes very affluent. If Mars occupies lagna conjoined or aspected by Mercury, Venus and Saturn, then also one becomes very rich.
