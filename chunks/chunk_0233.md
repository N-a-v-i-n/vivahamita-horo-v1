#### **10.6 Virodhargala (Obstruction)** 

Virodhargala shows the obstruction of argala. Planets and houses in the 12<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> houses from a house or planet cause virodhargala and obstruct the argala on it from the 2<sup>nd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 11<sup>th</sup> and 5<sup>th</sup> houses from it ( _respectively_ ). For example, let us say Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn are in Ge, Pi, Ar and Vi respectively. Saturn is in the 4<sup>th</sup> from Mercury and Ge and he causes argala on them. With Saturn being a malefic, this is a paapaargala (malefic intervention). But Jupiter is in the 10<sup>th</sup> from Mercury and Ge. So he obstructs Saturn’s argala and averts the troubles.
