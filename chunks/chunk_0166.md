In addition to the above list, we can find a house with respect to the _naisargika karaka_ (natural significator) who signifies the matter shown by a house. For example, Venus signifies vehicles. D-16 is the chart that shows vehicles and pleasures. So the 4<sup>th</sup> from Venus in D-16 can show one’s happiness from vehicles and other luxuries. As Mercury is the natural significator of speech, the 2<sup>nd</sup> house from lagna and the 2<sup>nd</sup> house from Mercury show speech (in rasi, D-9 and D-27<sup>14</sup> ). A complete list of naisargika karakas of different matters can be found in the chapter on “Karakas”. 

#### **7.4 Special Categories** 

There are some special categories of houses: 

- (1) The 1<sup>st</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> houses form a triangle and they are known as “konas” or “trikonas” or **“trines”** . 

- (2) The 1<sup>st</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> houses are called “kendras” or **“quadrants”** or “angles”. 

- (3) The 2<sup>nd</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses are called **“panapharas”** or “succedants”. These are basically the quadrants from the 2<sup>nd</sup> house.
