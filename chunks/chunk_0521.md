One may wonder why we are looking at antardasa and not mahadasa. Events shown by mahadasa suggest the overall mood of the whole 10 year period, while events shown by antardasa are applicable to 10 months here. With Li being the 4<sup>th</sup> house and having the argala of lord Venus, the native had the comfort of vehicles in Li dasa. However, Ta antardasa was bad due to Rahu and A8. Based on the event of interest, we should judiciously choose mahadasa or antardasa or pratyantardasa for examination. If an event plays a role in a native’s life for just a week, it is probably shown in pratyantardasa. If an event plays a role in a native’s life for a few months, it is probably shown in antardasa. Mahadasa shows the mood of longer periods. If we want to analyze a temporary activity that remains in a native’s mind for just an hour, it is probably seen in the praana-antardasa or deha-antardasa running then. We should determine the effective period of an event and judiciously choose the dasa division in which it should be seen.
