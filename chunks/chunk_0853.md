If not, how can we correlate Mercury’s retrogression and the typing mistakes? Probably the mistakes happen all the time, but they are more frequent during a few days. But the reason has to be a sookshma dasa or a praana dasa or a Bhrigu transit during that time, rather than Mercury’s retrogression. 

We should look at the nature of the event and the time period of its effectiveness and look for an astrological factor with about the same period of effectiveness. One may make typing mistakes everyday, but a particular day may be worse than usual. In that case, the period of effectiveness of the event ( _viz_ making too many typing mistakes) is that day. A month-long retrogression or a year-long transit cannot be its reason. If we correlate it with an astrological factor with the same period of effectiveness ( _e.g._ a sookshma dasa of Narayana dasa or Mercury’s D-27 transit in a sign occupied in natal rasi chart by Rahu), it is plausible. But if we attribute the event to retrograde Mercury or debilitated Jupiter, it is not logical.
