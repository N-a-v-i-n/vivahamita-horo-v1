If Trishoolas don’t bring death, the rasi containing Rudra in the 12<sup>th</sup> house can bring death. 

Antardasa at the time of death can be the 6<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> or 12<sup>th</sup> rasi from dasa rasi. It can also be a rasi that aspects the rasi that, in navamsa, contains the owner of the 8<sup>th</sup> house from dasa rasi. Suppose dasa rasi at death is Ta and Jupiter is in Le in navamsa. Then the antardasas of the 6<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses from Ta ( _i.e._ Li, Sc, Sg and Ar) can bring death. Also the antardasas of Ar, Le, Li and Cp – rasis that aspect Le, which contains Jupiter in navamsa – can bring death. If there is a common rasi between these two principles, it can be a strong candidate.
