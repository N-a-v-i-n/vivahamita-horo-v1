The 3<sup>rd</sup> house shows one’s boldness. The 3<sup>rd</sup> house from lagna and the 3<sup>rd</sup> house from Mars show one’s true boldness and the 3<sup>rd</sup> house from AL shows how bold one is perceived. In addition, A3 shows the maya surrounding one’s boldness. It shows what drives the perceptions of people about one’s boldness. The weapons one possesses may drive those perceptions and A3 may show one’s weapons.
