# **Part 6: Real-life Examples** 

The “exercises” one encounters in real life can be considerably more complicated than the tailor-made exercises given in this book. The exercises given in this book familiarize the reader with various techniques, but making real-life predictions requires more than that. 

To illustrate the process of making predictions, some real-life situations that this author faced in his experience will be given. The process of synthesizing a prediction will be illustrated in as much detail as possible. These examples are meant for giving some guidance and inspiration to those who are new to the process of making predictions.
