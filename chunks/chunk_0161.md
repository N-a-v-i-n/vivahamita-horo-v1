Lagna shows the _concept_ of self and it deals with one’s true _personality_ . The physical _existence_ of the person is different from this conceptual self. This applies to all divisional charts. Let us take an example. The 5<sup>th</sup> house shows scholarship, memory and success in competition. All these are related to learning and they are seen in D-24, the chart of learning.
