#### **14.5 The Eighth Lord Method** 

Parasara and Jaimini prescribed another method for estimating the longevity category. Find the stronger of lagna and 7<sup>th</sup> house and take it as the reference. Find the 8<sup>th</sup> lord from it and see where he is placed from it. If he is in a quadrant, long life is indicated. If he is in a panaphara, middle life is indicated. If he is in an apoklima, short life is indicated. 

**Example 48:** Let us say that lagna is in Ar and Li is stronger than Ar. Then we take Li as the reference. The 8<sup>th</sup> lord is Venus. If Venus is in Li, Cp, Ar or Cn ( _i.e._ a quadrant from Li), it shows long life. If Venus is in Sc, Aq, Ta or Le ( _i.e._ a panaphara from Li), it shows middle life. If Venus is in Sg, Pi, Ge or Vi ( _i.e._ an apoklima from Li), it shows short  life.
