This chart is also called **Saptavimsamsa** or **Bhamsa** . 

**Example 23:** Let us say Mercury is at 11° in Ge and Jupiter is at 19° in Sc. We see that 11° is in the 10<sup>th</sup> part of the rasi and 19° is in the 18<sup>th</sup> part of the rasi. Because Ge is an airy rasi, counting starts from Li. The 10<sup>th</sup> from Li is Le. So the 10<sup>th</sup> part in Ge goes into Le in D-27. On the other hand, Sc is a watery sign and counting starts from Cp. The 18<sup>th</sup> from Cp is Ge (18<sup>th</sup> = 6<sup>th</sup> , after removing 12). So the 18<sup>th</sup> part of Sc goes into Ge in D-27. So Mercury is in Le and Jupiter is in Ge in D-27 for this example. 

###### **6.2.17** **_Trimsamsa Chart (D-30)_** 

D-30 positions of planets are computed based on the following rules: 

###### <u>Odd Rasis:</u> 

- Bodies in 0°-5° in odd rasis are placed in Ar in D-30. 

- Bodies in 5°-10° in odd rasis are placed in Aq in D-30. 

- Bodies in 10°-18° in odd rasis are placed in Sg in D-30. 

- Bodies in 18°-25° in odd rasis are placed in Ge in D-30. 

- Bodies in 25°-30° in odd rasis are placed in Li in D-30.
