The 4<sup>th</sup> house stands for education. The 2<sup>nd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> from the 4<sup>th</sup> house are 5<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 2<sup>nd</sup> houses respectively. One’s intelligence (5<sup>th</sup> ), interaction with others (7<sup>th</sup> ) and and overall character and _samskara_ (2<sup>nd</sup> ) make or break one education. If Jupiter is in 5<sup>th</sup> house, he will give intelligence and his subhargala (benefic intervention) on 4<sup>th</sup> will help one’s education.
