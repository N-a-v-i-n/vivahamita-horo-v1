#### **18.4 Interpretation** 

Narayana dasa gives the progression of lagna in life. During the dasa of a rasi, that rasi acts as lagna. If dasas are started from the 7<sup>th</sup> house from lagna, then Narayana dasa gives the progression of the 7<sup>th</sup> house. So the 7<sup>th</sup> from dasa rasi gives the progressed lagna, _i.e._ the rasi that acts as lagna during the dasa. 

Treat the dasa rasi or the 7<sup>th</sup> from it – based on whether dasas start from lagna or the 7<sup>th</sup> house – as lagna and analyze the chart, to see fortune in a dasa. We will denote it with “dasa lagna”. We will denote the rasi containing the lord of dasa lagna with “paaka rasi”.
