###### **11.4.5** **_Hamsa Yoga_** 

**Definition:** If Jupiter is in a quadrant in own sign or exaltation sign, it is called Ruchaka yoga. In other words, Jupiter should be in Sg, Pi or Cn and he should be in 1<sup>st</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> or 10<sup>th</sup> from lagna. This yoga does not apply from Moon and it applies mainly in rasi chart. As an example, a native with lagna in Sg and Jupiter in Pi will have this yoga. 

**Results:** One born with this yoga is a great man of ethery nature. He is swan-like<sup>30</sup> . He has spiritual strength and purity. He is respected by everyone. He is very passionate. He becomes a king. He has all comforts. He enjoys life fully. He is a clever conversationalist and endowed with good speech.
