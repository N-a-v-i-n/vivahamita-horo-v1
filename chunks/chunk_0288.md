**Makuta Yoga:** If (1) Jupiter is in the 9<sup>th</sup> house from the 9<sup>th</sup> lord, (2) the 9<sup>th</sup> house from Jupiter has a benefic, and, (3) Saturn is in the 10<sup>th</sup> house, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is a powerful leader of men. He often manages unruly activities. Makuta means crown. 

**Jaya Yoga:** If (1) the 10<sup>th</sup> lord is in deep exaltation and (2) the 6<sup>th</sup> lord is debilitated, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is happy, successful, victorious over enemies and long-lived. Jaya means victorious. 

**Harsha Yoga:** If the 6<sup>th</sup> lord occupies the 6<sup>th</sup> house, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is happy, strong, good-natured and invincible. Harsha means joyous. 

**Sarala Yoga:** If the 8<sup>th</sup> lord occupies the 8<sup>th</sup> house, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is long-lived, fearless, learned, celebrated and prosperous. He is a terror to his enemies. Sarala means straight-forward.
