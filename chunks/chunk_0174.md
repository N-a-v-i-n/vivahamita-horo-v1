###### **7.4.4** 

###### **_Dusthanas_** 

> 16 We say that two planets are in “mutual trines”, if one planet is in a trine from the other. 

> 17 We say that two planets are in “mutual quadrants”, if one planet is in a quadrant from the other. 

Part 1: Chart Analysis 

77 

Dusthanas from a reference show forces causing setbacks to the matters signified by it. If a dusthana is fortified or afflicted by malefics, it may show serious obstacles. If a dusthana is weak, it shows that obstacles will be easily overcome. For example, exalted 8<sup>th</sup> lord may show a lot of troubles and debilitated 8<sup>th</sup> lord may show easy sailing.
