- (4) After this dasa, we go to the next rasi in the nine rasis. When we finish the nine rasis of the nakshatra pada, we go to the nine rasis of the next nakshatra pada. After the nine rasis of the 4<sup>th</sup> pada of a nakshatra, we go to the nine rasis corresponding to the 1<sup>st</sup> pada of the next nakshatra ( _i.e._ 1<sup>st</sup> pada of the constellations belonging to the _other sub-group_ in the _same group_ ). For example, after the nine rasis of the 4<sup>th</sup> pada of Savya-1 constellations, we go to the nine rasis of the 1<sup>st</sup> pada of Savya-2 constellations. After the nine rasis of the 4<sup>th</sup> pada of Apasavya-2 constellations, we go to the nine rasis of the 1<sup>st</sup> pada of Apasavya1 constellations.
