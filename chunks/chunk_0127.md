###### **6.2.12** **_Dwadasamsa Chart (D-12)_** 

Each rasi is divided into 12 equal parts of 2° 30' each. Bodies in the 12 parts of a rasi go into the 12 rasis starting from the rasi itself. 

**Example 19:** Let us say Mercury is at 11° in Ge and Jupiter is at 19° in Sc. We see that 11° is in the 5<sup>th</sup> part of the rasi and 19° is in the 8<sup>th</sup> part of the rasi. The 5<sup>th</sup> from Ge is Li. So the 5<sup>th</sup> part in Ge goes into Li in D-12. The 8<sup>th</sup> from Sc is Ge. So the 8<sup>th</sup> part of Sc goes into Ge in D-12. So Mercury is in Li and Jupiter is in Ge in D-12 for this example.
