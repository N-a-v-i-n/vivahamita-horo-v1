The lords of trines from lagna are functional benefics. The lords of 3<sup>rd</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> are functional malefics. The lord of a quadrant is a functional malefic if he is a natural benefic and functionally neutral if he is a natural malefic. The lords of 2<sup>nd</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> are functionally neutral. Of these, the 8<sup>th</sup> house is more malefic than the other two. 

Planet owning a quadrant and a trine becomes a yogakaraka (excellent planet).
