**Sringaataka Yoga:** If all the planets occupy trines (1<sup>st</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> ) from lagna, this yoga is formed. One born with this yoga is happy and liked by kings. He has a noble wife and hates women. He is wealthy. Sringaataka means a cross-road junction. It has some other popular meanings too.

**Hala Yoga:** If all the planets occupy mutual trines but not trines from lagna, this yoga is formed. In other words, if all the planets are in 2<sup>nd</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> or if all the planets are in 3<sup>rd</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> or if all the planets are in 4<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> , then this yoga is formed. One born with this yoga becomes a farmer. He eats a lot of food. He is poor. He is deserted by friends and relatives. He is unhappy and worried. Hala means a plough.
