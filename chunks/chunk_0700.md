- (4) Saturn has latta on the 8<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the forward direction. If Saturn is in Krittika, for example, he has latta on the 8<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from Krittika, _i.e._ Makha. 

###### **Prishtha latta (backward kick):** 

- (5) Moon has latta on the 22<sup>nd</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the backward direction. If Moon is in Anuradha, for example, he has latta on the 22<sup>nd</sup> nakshatra (backwards) from Anuradha, _i.e._ Dhanishtha. 

- (6) Mercury has latta on the 7<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the backward direction. If Mercury is in Punarvasu, for example, he has latta on the 7<sup>th</sup> nakshatra (backwards) from Punarvasu, _i.e._ Aswini. 

- (7) Venus has latta on the 5<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the backward direction. If Venus is in Mrigasira, for example, he has latta on the 5<sup>th</sup> nakshatra (backwards) from Mrigasira, _i.e._ Aswini. 

- (8) Rahu has latta on the 9<sup>th</sup> nakshatra from him, reckoned in the backward direction. If Rahu is in Punarvasu, for example, he has latta on the 9<sup>th</sup> nakshatra (backwards) from Punarvasu, _i.e._ Uttarabhadrapada.
