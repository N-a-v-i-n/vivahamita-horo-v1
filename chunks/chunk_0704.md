There are 9 x 9 = 81 squares in the chart. Middle 7 squares on the outer borders contains 7 nakshatras each. Abhijit (the last quarter of Uttarashadha) is included among the nakshatras. The innermost 5 squares contains weekdays and tithis (lunar days). The notation regarding tithis is: 

(1) The entry Nanda represents 1<sup>st</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> , 11<sup>th</sup> , 16<sup>th</sup> , 21<sup>st</sup> and 26<sup>th</sup> tithis.

(3) The entry Jaya represents 3<sup>rd</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> , 13<sup>th</sup> , 18<sup>th</sup> , 23<sup>rd</sup> and 28<sup>th</sup> tithis. 

(4) The entry Rikta represents 4<sup>th</sup> , 9<sup>th</sup> , 14<sup>th</sup> , 19<sup>th</sup> , 24<sup>th</sup> and 29<sup>th</sup> tithis. 

- (5) The entry Poorna represents 5<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> , 15<sup>th</sup> , 20<sup>th</sup> and 30<sup>th</sup> tithis.
