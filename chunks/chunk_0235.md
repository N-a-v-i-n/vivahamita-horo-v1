Argala from the 2<sup>nd</sup> house shows the basic ingredient for the sustenance of a matter. For example, the 2<sup>nd</sup> house shows food and it is a basic ingredient for the sustenance of self (1<sup>st</sup> ). The 5<sup>th</sup> house shows intelligence and it is a basic ingredient for the sustenance of learning (4<sup>th</sup> ). 

Argala from the 4<sup>th</sup> house shows the basic factor that drives the mood, state and progress of a matter. For example, the 4<sup>th</sup> house shows comfort and it drives the mood and state of self (1<sup>st</sup> ). The 7<sup>th</sup> house shows interaction and that drives one’s learning (4<sup>th</sup> ).
