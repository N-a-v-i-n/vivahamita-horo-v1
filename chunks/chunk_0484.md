###### **Ketu exception:** 

If Ketu occupies dasa seed, the basic direction of dasa progression becomes reversed. If it is normally forward, it becomes backward. If it is normally backward, it becomes forward. If Sc is dasa seed and it contains Ketu, dasa progression becomes Sc, Ar, Vi, Aq, Cn, Sg _etc_ (comparing this with Example 63, readers may note that the 6<sup>th</sup> house is counted in the forward direction instead of the backward direction).
