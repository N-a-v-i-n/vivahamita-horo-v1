###### **16.5.2** **_Using Dasa Variations_** 

If the sign containing the 5<sup>th</sup> star from Moon is stronger than the sign containing Moon, Vimsottari dasa started from utpanna star may be preferred for general results. If the 5<sup>th</sup> star spans across 2 signs, take the sign containing the same quarter as occupied by Moon in birthstar. If Moon is in Makha 3<sup>rd</sup> pada (quarter), for example, 5<sup>th</sup> star is Chitra and it starts in Virgo and ends in Libra. So we should take the 3<sup>rd</sup> quarter of Chitra and we then get Libra. So Leo’s strength should be compared to Libra’s. 

How do we know which sign is stronger? There are no clear guidelines in the literature to compare the strengths. A sign aspected by Jupiter and occupied by more planets may be taken to be stronger. We can also use known events to see which dasa is working better.
