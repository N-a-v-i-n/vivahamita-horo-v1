_Method of three pairs_ : (1) Lagna lord and 8<sup>th</sup> lord is Venus and he is in dual rasi. Dual + Dual gives middle life. (2) Moon is in a fixed rasi and Saturn is in a dual rasi. Fixed + Dual gives long life. (3) Lagna and HL are in movable rasis. Movable + Movable gives long life. So the final result is “long life”. This range covers 72-108 years. So the last 4 Shoola dasas can bring death. 

_Shoola dasa_ : The 7<sup>th</sup> house Aries is stronger as it has the aspect of Sun, Mercury and its lord Mars. Dasas start from Ar and go as Ar, Ta, Ge, Cn _etc_ . The first 8 dasas are over after 72 years (8 x 9). The 9<sup>th</sup> dasa of Sg runs during 72-81 years of age. The 10<sup>th</sup> dasa of Cp runs during 81-90 years of age. The 11<sup>th</sup> dasa of Aq runs during 9099 years of age. The 12<sup>th</sup> dasa of Pi runs during 99-108 years of age. These are the four dasas in the long life range.
