Thus one should adapt the rules in the case of retrogression. Even when there is no retrogression, some changes may have to be made. Suppose Mercury is at 18º in Vi and Mars is at 18°45'  in Cp and neither is retrograde. Then one may be tempted to think that there is a poorna ithasala yoga in their trinal aspect. Now suppose that Mercury is about to become retrograde and he will become retrograde after reaching 18°05'. In that case, Mercury and Mars are not going to have an _exact_ aspect anytime soon, because Mercury will start going backward after reaching 18°05'. So there is no ithasala. 

Thus an erudite astrologer should make proper modifications to the rules when a planet is retrograde or about to become retrograde.
