**Example 86:** Let us consider India Gandhi’s rasi chart given in Chart 61 (in a later chapter). She was assassinated on October 31, 1984. 

Because Saturn is in Cn, we take the 8<sup>th</sup> houses from Cn and Cp in the normal way, instead of using Table 32. The 8<sup>th</sup> lord Rahu is debilitated and he joins another planet. The 2<sup>nd</sup> lord Sun also joins another planet. But debilitated 8<sup>th</sup> lord is a better candidate for being Rudra. So Trishoola is in the trines from Sg, _i.e._ Ar, Le and Sg. 

Because the 2<sup>nd</sup> house with a planet is stronger than the empty 8<sup>th</sup> house, dasas start from the 2<sup>nd</sup> house. As Le is an odd rasi, dasas go zodiacally. Dasas go as Le, Vi, Sc, Sg _etc_ . It may be verified that Ar dasa runs during Nov 1982-Nov 1989. Ar is a Trishoola rasi.
