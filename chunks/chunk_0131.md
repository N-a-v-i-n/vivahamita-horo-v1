###### **6.2.16** **_Nakshatramsa Chart (D-27)_** 

Each rasi is divided into 27 equal parts of 1° 6' 40'' each. Bodies in the 27 parts of a rasi go into the 12 rasis starting from Ar, Cn, Li and Cp based on whether the rasi is a fiery, earthy, airy or watery rasi. 

This chart is also called **Saptavimsamsa** or **Bhamsa** .
