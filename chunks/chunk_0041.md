In solar calendar, one year is the time in which Sun moves by 360° and one month is the time in which Sun moves by 30°. These are called “solar year” and “solar month” respectively. Each solar month has 30 days, where one day stands for exactly 1° motion of Sun). This calendar will be used in dasas and in Tajaka analysis.
