**Example 63:** Suppose someone has lagna in Ta. Suppose Sc is stronger than Ta. Let us find the dasa progression. 

Because the 7<sup>th</sup> house is stronger than lagna, it becomes the dasa seed. So the dasa seed is Sc. Because it is a fixed sign, we use “every 6<sup>th</sup> ” progression governed by Shiva. The 9<sup>th</sup> house from Sc is Cn. It is an even-footed sign. So the overall direction is backward. So we should count houses in the backward direction. The 6<sup>th</sup> house from Sc, counted backwards, is Ge. The 6<sup>th</sup> therefrom is Cp. We count the 6<sup>th</sup> house from every rasi in the backward direction and get the complete dasa sequence as: 

Sc, Ge, Cp, Le, Pi, Li, Ta, Sg, Cn, Aq, Vi and Ar 

This is the order of dasas for the native.
