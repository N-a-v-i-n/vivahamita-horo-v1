- (6) The resulting sign contains the arudha pada of the house of interest. 

Arudha pada of a house is simply called arudha or pada also. In this book, we will denote the arudha pada on _n_<sup>th</sup> house with A _n_ . For example, arudha pada of 4<sup>th</sup> house is A4 and arudha pada of 9<sup>th</sup> house is A9. 

There are two special cases: Arudha pada of lagna is denoted as AL (arudha lagna) and arudha pada of 12<sup>th</sup> house is denoted as UL (upapada lagna).
