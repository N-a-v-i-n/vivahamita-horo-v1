#### **19.2 Computation** 

- (1) Dasas start from the stronger of lagna and 7<sup>th</sup> house. Let us call this rasi “dasa seed”. 

- (2) The direction of reckoning dasas is forward or backward based on whether lagna is in an odd sign or an even sign. If Saturn is in the stronger of lagna and 7<sup>th</sup> , dasa order is forward. If Ketu is in the stronger of lagna and 7<sup>th</sup> , dasa order is reversed. 

NOTE: We are talking about _odd_ and _even_ signs here and _not_ about odd-footed and even-footed signs. 

- (3) First 4 dasas will belong to the kendras (1<sup>st</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 7<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> ) from dasa seed. 

- (4) Next 4 dasas will belong to the panapharas (2<sup>nd</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> ) from dasa seed.
