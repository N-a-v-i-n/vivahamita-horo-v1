## **31. Sudarsana Chakra Dasa** 

#### **31.1 Introduction** 

Parasara said that Sudarsana Chakra dasa was taught by Brahma – the Creator – Himself and that it is a great tool for predicting daily, monthly and annual fortune. Because the calculations necessary for Sudarsana Chakra dasa interpretation are an integral part of casting Tajaka annual, monthly and sixty-hour charts, this dasa is being covered in the part on “Tajaka Analysis” rather than the part on “Dasa Analysis”. However, it should be borne in mind that this is a dasa applicable to natal charts. This is just a natal dasa like Vimsottari dasa, Narayana dasa and Kalachakra dasa.
