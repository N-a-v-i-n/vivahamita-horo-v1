Cp, Sg, Sc, Li, Vi, Le, Cn, Ge, Ta, Ar, Pi and Aq. 

This is the order of dasas for the native. 

###### **Saturn exception:** 

If Saturn occupies dasa seed, dasa progression becomes regular and zodiacal. If Sc is dasa seed and it contains Saturn, dasa progression becomes Sc, Sg, Cp, Aq, Pi, Ar _etc_ . If Pi is dasa seed and it contains Saturn, dasa progression becomes Pi, Ar, Ta, Ge, Cn, Le _etc_ . We basically make the direction “forward” and use Brahma’s progression.
