Computing ashtakavarga by hand is not as difficult as it may seem to be. In fact, even today, some people in India compute all the ashtakavarga tables by hand in a matter of a few minutes. 

**Example 37:** Let us say Venus is in Ge. Find the rasis in which Jupiter is benefic with respect to Venus. 

To find the rasis in which Jupiter is benefic, we should look at Jupiter’s ashtakavarga (see Table 23). To find the rasis in which Jupiter is benefic with respect to Venus, we should look at the column of Venus. Only the 2<sup>nd</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> , 6<sup>th</sup> , 9<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses have a 1 (rekha – benefic point) against them.
