- (5) Jupiter is in Ar. He owns 2 signs – Sg and Pi. Pi is stronger as it has 3 planets and Sg is empty. From Ar, Pi is the 12<sup>th</sup> sign. Counting 12 signs from Pi, we get Aq. So Aq contains Jupiter’s arudha pada. 

- (6) Venus is in Pi. He owns 2 signs – Ta and Li. Li is stronger (it will become clear after reading the chapter on “Strength of Planets and Rasis”.). From Pi, Li is the 8<sup>th</sup> sign. Counting 8 signs from Li, we get Ta. So Ta contains the arudha pada of Venus. 

- (7) Saturn is in Ar. He owns 2 signs – Aq and Cp. Cp is stronger as it has Ketu and Aq is empty. From Ar, Cp is the 10<sup>th</sup> sign. Counting 10 signs from Cp, we get Li. Since Li is the 7<sup>th</sup> from Ar, we take the 10<sup>th</sup> from Li and get Cn. So Cn contains Saturn’s arudha pada. 

- (8) Rahu is in Cn. From Cn, Rahu’s own sign Aq is the 8<sup>th</sup> sign. Counting 8 signs from Aq, we get Vi. So Vi contains Rahu’s arudha pada. 

- (9) Ketu is in Cp. From Cp, Ketu’s own sign Sc is the 11<sup>th</sup> sign. Counting 11 signs from Sc, we get Vi. So Vi contains Ketu’s arudha pada.
