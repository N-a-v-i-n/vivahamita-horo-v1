Narayana dasa specializes in showing what happens in one’s life; Vimsottari dasa specializes in showing how one’s mind _views_ what happens in one’s life; and, Kalachakra dasa specializes in showing how one _relates to_ what happens in one’s life and how connected one feels. These three are the most important of all general <u>purpose phalita dasas.</u> 

Different dasas do not provide different alternatives that can be used _interchangeably_ to understand what happens in one’s life, but they provide different angles – or vantage points – to view the same kaleidoscope that life is.
