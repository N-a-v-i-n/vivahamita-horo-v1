The stand of this book is very clear – each bhava (house) with respect to one reference can only be in one rasi. Even if Saturn is at 1º in Vi and lagna is at 29º in Le, we still say that the 1<sup>st</sup> house is in Le, the 2<sup>nd</sup> house is in Li and Saturn is in the 2<sup>nd</sup> house (though he is only 2º away from lagna). We do not recognize the house division methods of Porphyry and others in this book. Each rasi is a house and the 1<sup>st</sup> house is the rasi containing the reference. Readers will do well to follow Maharshi Parasara and ignore the creations and borrowings of later day Indian astrologers.
