## **21. Drigdasa** 

#### **21.1 Introduction** 

Drigdasa is a rasi dasa. Drik means vision and drigdasa is a dasa based on aspects. It shows how spiritual vision develops in a native and steers one’s life. If a native’s chart promises spiritual growth, this dasa shows religious and spiritual activities and the evolution of one’s soul. 

#### **21.2 Computation** 

- (1) Dasas start from the 9<sup>th</sup> house. 

- (2) First 4 dasas belong to the 9<sup>th</sup> house and the 3 signs aspected by it. Order of reckoning is forward or backward based on whether the 9<sup>th</sup> house is odd-footed or even-footed. 

- (3) Next 4 dasas belong to the next house (10<sup>th</sup> house) and the 3 signs aspected by it. Order of reckoning is forward or backward based on whether the 10<sup>th</sup> house is odd-footed or even-footed. 

- (4) Last 4 dasas belong to the house after that (11<sup>th</sup> house) and the 3 signs aspected by it. Order of reckoning is forward or backward based on whether the 11<sup>th</sup> house is odd-footed or even-footed. 

- (5) Dasa periods of various rasis in this dasa system are found just like in Narayana dasa.
