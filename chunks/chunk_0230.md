Let us take another example. Short journeys are seen from the 3<sup>rd</sup> house. What are the things necessary for a journey? One needs a vehicle (4<sup>th</sup> ). One may need someone to serve one by driving the vehicle. Servants are seen from 6<sup>th</sup> . So, no wonder the 4<sup>th</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> houses cause argala on the 3<sup>rd</sup> house, being the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 4<sup>th</sup> from it respectively! If one has papargala on 3<sup>rd</sup> from 4<sup>th</sup> , it may show trouble to the journey because of the vehicle. If one has papargala on 3<sup>rd</sup> from 6<sup>th</sup> , it may show trouble to the journey because of the driver.
