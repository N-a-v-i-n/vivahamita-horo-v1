Sometimes we cannot explain new observations on the basis of old models and we have to come up with new theories and postulates. We may not be able to explain the influence of planets on us on the basis of the scientific model of the universe we have today. But that does not trivialize the statistical study of the correlations between planetary combinations and events in the lives of individuals.
