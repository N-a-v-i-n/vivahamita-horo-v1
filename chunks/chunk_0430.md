- (4) A rasi whose lord is in a rasi with a different oddity (odd/even) is stronger than a rasi whose lord is in a rasi with the same oddity. Suppose we are compring the strengths of Ar and Li. Suppose we have a tie after rule (3). Suppose Venus is in Cn, an even rasi and Mars is in Le, an odd rasi. Both Ar and Li are odd rasis. Because the lord of Li is in an even rasi ( _i.e._ a rasi with a _different_ oddity) and the lord of Ar is in an odd rasi ( _i.e._ a rasi with _the same_ oddity), Li is stronger. 

NOTE: If we have a tie upto rule (3) when finding the stronger rasi owned by a planet for computing its graha arudha, this rule will surely resolve the tie, because the two rasis owned by each planet have a different oddity. So one of them will have the lord in a rasi with a different oddity.
