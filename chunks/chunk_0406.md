After we find the avastha index, we can multiply it with itself and add the number corresponding to the first sound in the first syllable of the name, as found from Table 37. We can take the remainder when that number is divided by 12. Then we can add a planetary adjustment to the remainder. We use 5 for Sun and Jupiter, 2 Moon and Mars, 3 for Mercury, Venus and Saturn and 4 for Rahu and Ketu. We divide the sum by 3 and take the remainder. This gives the strength of the activity. A value of 1 means “drishti” and medium results. A value of 2 means “cheshta” and full results. A value of 3 (or 0) means “vicheshta” and gives very little results. Based on the activity-related state of a planet, we can guess the results given by it. Based on whether it is in Cheshta, Drishti or Vicheshta, the results will be full, medium or little.
