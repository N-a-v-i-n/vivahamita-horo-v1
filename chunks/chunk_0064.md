- (1) Ar, Le and Sg are called _agni_ rasis or _fiery_ rasis. 

- (2) Ta, Vi and Cp are called _bhoo_ rasis or _earthy_ rasis. 

- (3) Ge, Li and Aq are called _vaayu_ rasis or _airy_ rasis. 

- (4) Cn, Sc and Pi are called _jala_ rasis or _watery_ rasis. 

- (5) The 5<sup>th</sup> element of _aakaasa_ or _ether_ is present in every rasi. 

Let us see how these may be used. For example, the 5<sup>th</sup> house in one’s chart shows one’s emotional nature. The 5<sup>th</sup> house in a fiery sign may show a normally angry, aggressive or determined person. The 5<sup>th</sup> house in an earthy sign may show a balanced, logical and stable person. The 5<sup>th</sup> house in an airy sign may show someone with unstable and wandering emotions. The 5<sup>th</sup> house in a watery sign may show one with an imaginative and creative mind.
