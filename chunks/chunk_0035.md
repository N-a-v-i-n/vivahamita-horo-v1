Out of the three chart formats, (1) and (3) are rasi-based and (2) is bhava-based. In rasi-based chart drawing formats, a rasi is always at a fixed position. Ar is always in one particular position and Ta is in another position and so on. Planets, lagna _etc_ are placed in the box (or the visual area) representing the rasi occupied by it. In bhavabased chart drawing formats, a bhava (house) is always at a fixed position. Lagna (denoted by “Asc” for ascendant) is always in a particular visual area of the chart and the 2<sup>nd</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> _etc_ houses are in fixed positions.
