**Definition:** Budha means Mercury, Aaditya means Sun and yoga means togetherness. If Sun and Mercury are together (in one sign), this yoga is present. 

**Results:** One born with this yoga is intelligent and skillful in all works. He is wellknown, respected and happy. Nipuna means an expert. 

**Note:** If Mercury is too close to Sun, he is combust ( _asta_ or _astangata_ ). Yogas formed by combust planets lose some of their power to do good. This yoga is the most powerful in divisional charts like D-10. In rasi chart also, it can give results if Mercury is not combust. Suppose someone has Sun and Mercury together in Leo in D-10. Then that person has a powerful Budha-Aditya yoga in career. The results will be felt throughout one’s life and the periods of Sun, Mercury and Leo will give those results in particular.
