**Example 27:** Let us look at the chart of Bill Cosby. He is a famous TV and film actor in USA. He is one of the greatest theatrical personalities of the world and a natural actor. He is known as a charitable person who donated huge amounts of money for the education of African American kids. His birthdata is: 12<sup>th</sup> July 1937, 12:30 am (Time Zone: 5:00 west of GMT), 75 W 10, 39 N 57. 

The 9<sup>th</sup> house shows charitable nature and the 12<sup>th</sup> house shows expenditure. Jupiter owns the 9<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses and occupies the 9<sup>th</sup> house here. This may be the reason for Mr. Cosby’s charitable nature. Let us see the amsabala of Jupiter. Jupiter is at 29° 49' in Sg. The signs occupied by him in various charts are shown below: 

_Rasi:_ Sg, _D-2:_ Cn, _D-3:_ Li, _D-4:_ Vi, _D-7:_ Ge, _D-9:_ Sg, _D-10:_ Vi, _D-12:_ Sc, _D-16:_ Pi, _D-20:_ Pi, _D-24:_ Cn, _D-27:_ Ge, _D-30:_ Li, _D-40:_ Cn, _D-45:_ Le, _D-60:_ Sc. 

Jupiter owns Sg and Pi. His moolatrikona is Sg. He is exalted in Cn. So we have to count the charts in which he is in Cn, Sg or Pi.
