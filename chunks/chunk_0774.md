###### **29.2.9** **_Gairi-Kamboola Yoga_** 

If Moon is in the last degree of a rasi and he will form ithasala with one of the planets in an ithasala after moving to the next rasi, it is called Gairi-Kamboola yoga. It is essentially Kamboola yoga in waiting. Moon must also form an ithasala with a strong planet for this yoga to be effective. Moreover, Moon should not be exalted or debilitated or occupy his own navamsa, drekkana or hadda. This yoga shows realization of the matter with help from others, after some delay.
