Ketu occupies Le and the exceptions don’t apply. Dasa sequence is — Le, Cp, Ge, Sc, Ar, Vi, Aq, Cn, Sg, Ta, Li, Pi. Let us now find the lengths of various dasas. 

Le is an even-footed sign. So we count houses from Le to its lord Sun in the backward direction. Sun is in Cn, _i.e._ the 2<sup>nd</sup> house from Le in the backward direction. So dasa length is 2–1=1 year. The native was born in August 1912. This dasa runs from Aug 1912 to Aug 1913. 

Cp is an even-footed sign. So we count houses from Cp to its lord Saturn in the backward direction. Saturn is in Ta, _i.e._ the 9<sup>th</sup> house from Cp in the backward direction. So dasa length is 9–1=8 years. This dasa runs from Aug 1913 to Aug 1921. 

Ge is an odd-footed sign. So we count houses from Ge to its lord Mercury in the forward direction. Mercury is in Le, _i.e._ the 3<sup>rd</sup> house from Ge in the forward direction. So dasa length is 3–1=2 years. This dasa runs from Aug 1921 to Aug 1923.
