- (6) Pi has 1 planet and Vi is empty. Pi is stronger than Vi, from rule (1). 

# **Part 2: Dasa Analysis** 

One unique feature of Vedic astrology is its concept of “dasa” systems. Dasa means “a period”. One’s lifetime is divided into several periods, sub-periods, sub-sub-periods and so on. These are ruled by different planets or rasis. Planets and rasis give their results in their respective periods and sub-periods. Each dasa system has a specific use. The nature of results given by planets and rasis in their respective periods and sub-periods depends on the dasa system being used. 

There are many classifications of dasa systems: 

- **(1) Nakshatra dasas and Rasi dasas:** Nakshatra dasas are based on Moon’s nakshatra (constellation). Rasi dasas are based on rasis occupied by planets and dasas are owned by rasis in rasi dasas. 

- **(2) Phalita dasas and Ayur dasas:** Phalita means results and ayur means longevity. Phalita dasas are used for general results and ayur dasas are used for predicting death.
