##### **One Last Word** 

The purpose of these examples is not to brag about some correct predictions. Every astrologer makes hundreds or thousands of correct predictions and possibly hundreds of wrong predictions too. The purpose of the above examples is to illustrate the process of making predictions and to give guidance to those who are new to the process of making predictions.
