_Warning_ : The above rules are too general. One should understand the meaning of each rule and adapt based on the situation. For example, aspect of Mercury, Jupiter and lord is important for Narayana dasa and other phalita dasas. In ayur dasas that show longevity, aspect of luminaries (Sun and Moon) are important. Aspect of all other planets is equally important. There is no significance for the aspect of lord. In dasas based on AK ( _e.g._ Atmakaraka kendradi graha/rasi dasas), the sign containing AK is stronger than any other rasi. After we check for the aspects of Mercury, Jupiter and rasi lord, we should look at the placement of rasi lord from AK. A rasi who lord is in a quadrant from AK is stronger than a rasi whose lord is in a panaphara from AK. These rules, however, are not applicable to all dasas.
