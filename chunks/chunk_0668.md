For example, suppose we want to time the good and bad periods of a native’s father. Father should be seen from Sun and the 9<sup>th</sup> house. We can take Sun’s BAV and find the number of rekhas in the 9<sup>th</sup> house from Sun. Suppose Sun is in Aq. Suppose Sun’s BAV contains 5 rekhas in Li (the 9<sup>th</sup> from Aq). Suppose Sun’s sodhya pinda is 86. Multiplying 86 with 5, we get 430. If we divide 430 by 27, the quotient is 15 and the remainder is 25. The 25<sup>th</sup> constellation is Poorvabhadrapada. So Saturn’s transit in Poorvabhadrapada is bad for father and Jupiter’s transit in the same nakshatra is good. Now let us find the rasi. By dividing 430 by 12, we get a quotient of 35 and a remainder of 10. So Saturn’s transit in Cp (the 10<sup>th</sup> rasi of the zodiac) is bad for father and Jupiter’s transit in Cp is good.
