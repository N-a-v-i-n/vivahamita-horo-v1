Let us take one final example – Ketu. Ketu is most comfortable with occult activity, which are shown by the 8<sup>th</sup> house. So he owns the 8<sup>th</sup> house of the natural zodiac, _i.e._ Scorpio. His official duty, however, is giving upaasana (meditation) and moksha (liberation), which are shown by the 12<sup>th</sup> house. So his moolatrikona is in the 12<sup>th</sup> house of the natural zodiac, _i.e._ Pisces. 

One should remember the above and understand the mood of a planet based on whether it is in own rasi or exaltation rasi or moolatrikona. Though all the three are good placements, there is a subtle difference in the mood of the planet and the results given by it.
