#### **36.2 Definition of Muhurta** 

Asrologers set nice muhurtas for the tasks of their clients, but they don’t always verify that their clients know exactly what to do at the time of the muhurta. For example, if we set an auspicious muhurta for someone to join in a new job, what should he do at the time? Should he sign the job offer letter? Or, should he sign the employment contract (if applicable)? Or, should he sit in front of his computer and power it on? Or, should he read a document describing the work he has to do?
