#### **6.5 Using Divisional Charts** 

It is very important to memorize Table 11. We should choose the divisional chart to analyze, based on the matter we are interested in. If we want to know something about one’s career, for example, we should analyze one’s dasamsa chart (D-10). If we want to know something about one’s luxuries and pleasures, we should analyze one’s shodasamsa (D-16). Based on the matter of interest, we decide which area of life is relevant and analyze the corresponding divisional chart. 

We should remember which planets, rasis and houses show a particular matter and find links between them in the divisional chart of interest.
