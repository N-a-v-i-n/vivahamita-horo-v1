- (1) **Trinal aspect:** A planet has a strong benefic aspect on the 5<sup>th</sup> and 9<sup>th</sup> houses from it and on the planets in those houses. 

- (2) **Sextile aspect:** A planet has a weak benefic aspect on the 3<sup>rd</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses from it and on the planets in those houses. 

- (3) **Square aspect:** A planet has a weak malefic aspect on the 4<sup>th</sup> and 10<sup>th</sup> houses from it and on the planets in those houses. 

- (4) **Conjunction:** A planet has a strong malefic aspect on the planets in the same house occupied by it. 

- (5) **Opposition:** A planet has a strong malefic aspect on the 7<sup>th</sup> house from it and on the planets in that house. 

- (6) **Semi-sextile aspect:** A planet has a neutral aspect on the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses from it and on the planets in those houses.
