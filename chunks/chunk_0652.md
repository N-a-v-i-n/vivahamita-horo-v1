- (3) Mercury is the lord of lagna and occupies Cp, in the natal navamsa chart. He is in Cp in the transit rasi chart also. 

**Example 106:** Let us consider the natal D-7 chart of a gentleman and the transit rasi chart at the time of the birth of his daughter (see Chart 55). 

D-7 shows children and happiness from them. The 5<sup>th</sup> house shows children. It is Vi here. Jupiter, the significator of children, and exalted Mercury occupy it. It may be noticed that both Jupiter and Mercury are in Vi at the time of child-birth. So 5<sup>th</sup> lord in D-7 and significator of children activated the 5<sup>th</sup> house of D-7 in their rasi chart transit and that gave a child. 

**Example 107:** Let us consider the death of John F. Kennedy, Jr. Natal D-11 and transit rasi charts are given in Chart 56. Natal rasi chart and transit D-11 charts are given in Chart 57. 

D-11 or Rudramsa is the chart that shows death and destruction. Considering that the native had a violent death in a plane crash, D-11 is the apt chart to see his death.
