**Example 82:** Chart 37 gives the rasi chart of an ISKCON<sup>57</sup> devotee who was disillusioned with the materialistic world, left his education in mathematics at a Russian university, wandered in the forests for a few months and then found ISKCON. He was introduced to ISKCON and moved to a monastery in 1990. 

**Chart 37** 



Lagna is in Aq. The 9<sup>th</sup> house is in Li. It is an odd-footed sign. So we start from Li, go zodiacally and list the rasis aspecting Li. They are Li, Aq, Ta and Le. The 10<sup>th</sup> house is in Sc. It is an odd-footed sign. So we start from Sc, go zodiacally and list the rasis aspecting Sc. They are Sc, Cp, Ar and Cn. The 11<sup>th</sup> house is in Sg. It is an oddfooted sign. So we start from Sg, go zodiacally and list the rasis aspecting Sg. They are Sg, Pi, Ge and Vi. So dasas go as 

Li, Aq, Ta, Le, Sc, Cp, Ar, Cn, Sg, Pi, Ge, Vi
