Thus, we should pay attention if any planet is at a rasi border in the divisional chart of interest. If so, we must consider both the positions and see which one makes better sense based on known past. 

###### **Lagna:** 

Lagna changes rasi in divisional charts much faster than planets. So it is the most important consideration in birthtime rectification. 

Lagna moves by one rasi (30º) in 2 hours or 120 min. To move by 1º, lagna takes about 4 min. 

**_Lesson_ :** Lagna moves by 1º in 4 min. Lagna moves by 10' in 2/3 min (or 40 seconds). Lagna moves by 1' in 4 sec. Lagna moves by 10'' in 2/3 sec. 

We can see that lagna changes rasi in D-10 in 12 min. It changes rasi in D-24 in 5 min.
