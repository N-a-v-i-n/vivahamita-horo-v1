Let us look at India’s emperor Akbar<sup>39</sup> (see Chart 10). His chart has Libra lagna. Lagna lord Venus and 4<sup>th</sup> and 5<sup>th</sup> lord Saturn are in lagna. Due to all these reasons, Akbar was a great emperor of India although Raja Yoga giving planets weren’t in Simhaasanaamsa. We should always make the final judgment after looking at all the factors.
