One can notice that dasa sequences and antardasa sequences all come from the two 24-rasi sequences given in Kalachakra (see Table 43). In the cases of dasas, the starting point in the 24-rasi sequence is determined by the nakshatra pada of Moon and the portion elapsed in it. In the case of antardasas, the starting point is based on dasa rasi. 

To learn how the lengths of antardasas are computed, let us take Le dasa as an example. We found that antardasas in Le dasa are Le, Cn, Ge, Ta, Ar, Sg, Cp, Aq, Pi. By adding the dasa lengths of these 9 rasis (see Table 48), we get 86 years. So we divide Le dasa of 5 years into 86 parts and give 5 parts to Le antardasa, 21 parts to Cn antardasa, 9 parts to Ge antardasa and so on.
