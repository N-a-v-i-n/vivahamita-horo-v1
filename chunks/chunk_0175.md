###### **7.4.5** **_Visible Half and Invisible Half_** 

The zodiac is divided into two halves in every chart: The 7<sup>th</sup> , 8<sup>th</sup> , 9<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> , 11<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> houses form the “visible half” of the zodiac. The 1<sup>st</sup> , 2<sup>nd</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 5<sup>th</sup> and 6<sup>th</sup> houses form the “invisible half” of the zodiac. The houses in the visible half of the zodiac with respect to a reference give results that can be seen in the material world and the houses in the invisible half of the zodiac give results that cannot be easily seen. It is for this reason that the bases of dharma trikona (1<sup>st</sup> house) and moksha trikona (4<sup>th</sup> house) are in the invisible half and the bases of artha trikona (10<sup>th</sup> house) and kaama trikona (7<sup>th</sup> house) are in the visible half.
