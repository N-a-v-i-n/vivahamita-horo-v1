## **7. Houses** 

#### **7.1 Introduction** 

The zodiac consists of 12 rasis. Each rasi is said to form a house. When we talk about houses, we always have a point of reference. The rasi containing the point of reference is the 1<sup>st</sup> house. The next rasi is the 2<sup>nd</sup> house. The rasi after that is the 3<sup>rd</sup> house. Suppose Moon is in Aquarius and suppose we want houses with respect to Moon. Then Aquarius is the 1<sup>st</sup> house, Pisces is the 2<sup>nd</sup> house, Aries is the 3<sup>rd</sup> house, Taurus is the 4<sup>th</sup> house and so on. As we go around the zodiac, we reach Capricorn when we find the 12<sup>th</sup> house. 

In the same chart, Sun may be in Taurus. When we find houses with respect to Sun, Taurus contains the 1<sup>st</sup> house, Gemini contains the 2<sup>nd</sup> house, Cancer contains the 3<sup>rd</sup> house and so on. If Ghati Lagna is in Virgo in the same chart, then the 1<sup>st</sup> , 2<sup>nd</sup> and 3<sup>rd</sup> houses with respect to Ghati Lagna are in Virgo, Libra and Scorpio respectively.
