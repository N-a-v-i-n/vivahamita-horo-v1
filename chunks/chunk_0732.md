In other words, we can cast a chart after every 2º30' motion of Sun and use that Tajaka sixty-hour chart to predict events in the 2.5-day period commencing then. There are exactly 12 such charts in a one-month period. 

For example, let us take the second month of the year 2000-2001 for the native of Example 118. The second month commences when Sun enters 23° 50' 25'' in Pi, _i.e._ on 7<sup>th</sup> April 2000 at 10:38:06 am. The first shashti-hora of the month also starts then. It ends and the second shashti-hora or 60-hour period commences when Sun is advanced by 2º30', _i.e._ when Sun enters 26° 20' 25'' in Pi. This happens at 11:40:51 pm (IST) on 9<sup>th</sup> April 2000. If we cast a chart for that moment, we can make some predictions for the next 2.5 days.
