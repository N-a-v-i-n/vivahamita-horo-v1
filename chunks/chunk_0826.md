Thus, sometimes we cannot detect a birthtime error just by looking at the natal chart and dasas. Looking at the divisional charts of Tajaka annual charts can help in some cases. 

The assumption here is that one casts Tajaka charts very accurately. This is not true with those who use the approximate method taught in this boo or those who find the exact solar return but use an approximate formula for Sun’s motion or use a linear formula for ayanamsa.
