###### **2.2.7** **_Sattwa, Rajas and Tamas_** 

In Hindu philosophy, everything in this universe has one of 3 gunas (qualities). They are called trigunas. _Sattwa_ (purity) is a quality that gives truthfulness and purity. _Rajas_ or _rajo_ guna (energy) is a quality that makes one energetic and passionate. _Tamas_ or _tamo_ guna (darkness) is a quality that makes one depraved. 

As an example, let us say a saint is living in a small hermitage. Let us say a man living in a local forest comes, makes fun of the saint and abuses him for no reason. Let us say a king fights with that man after finding out about this. Let us say that the saint tells him to leave the man and yet the king punishes him.
