**Hara Yoga:** If benefics occupy the 4th, 9<sup>th</sup> and 8<sup>th</sup> houses counted from the 7<sup>th</sup> lord, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is happy, learned and blessed with wealth and children. Hara is a name of Lord Shiva. The 7<sup>th</sup> house rules death and Shiva is represented by its lord. This is why the 7<sup>th</sup> house shows genitalia and Shiva is worshipped in the form of a Linga – Phallus – in Hindu temples. 

**Brahma Yoga:** If benefics occupy the 4<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> and 11<sup>th</sup> houses counted from lagna lord, then this yoga is present. One born with this yoga is happy, learned and blessed with wealth and children. Brahma is the creator of this universe. Lagna rules birth and the Creator is represented in a chart by lagna lord. 

NOTE: (1) Some authors combine Hari yoga, Hara yoga and Brahma yoga and call it “Hari Hara Brahma yoga”. Also, these three yogas are known as Trimurthi Yogas. Brahma is the Creator; Hari is the Sustainer; and, Shiva is the Destroyer. They form the Trinity of Gods (Trimurthis).
