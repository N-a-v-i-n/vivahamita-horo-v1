**Example 128:** Let us consider the lady of Chart 74. She got married in January 1999. Let us analyze her Sudarsana Chakra dasa of navamsa for 1998-99. 

She was born in 1973 and she started her 26<sup>th</sup> year in July 1998. If we divide 26 by 12, we get a remainder of 2. So SC dasa in 1998-99 belongs to the 2<sup>nd</sup> house. In natal navamsa, lagna is in Cn and it is stronger than Moon and Sun. The 2<sup>nd</sup> house from Cn is Le. So Le dasa was running in 1998-99. Is it a good dasa? 

Three benefics are in quadrants and 3 malefics are in 3<sup>rd</sup> /11<sup>th</sup> . Though Mars is in 9<sup>th</sup> , he is strong being in own house. Most planets are favorably placed and, more importantly, lagna and 7<sup>th</sup> are strong with Jupiter and Venus in them (respectively). So this was a favorable year for matters shown by D-9.
