The rasis containing the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> houses are called **maraka sthanas** (killer stations). When we use rasi-ruled dasas that can show death, dasas of these rasis can bring death. Lords of the 2<sup>nd</sup> and 7<sup>th</sup> houses are called **maraka grahas** (killer planets). When we use planet-ruled dasas that can show death, dasas of these planets can bring death.
