There are a couple of exceptions: 

- (1) If the 8<sup>th</sup> lord from AK is in own rasi or exaltation rasi, then take the stronger of the 8<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> lords from him. Suppose AK is Mars and he is in Ge. Then the 8<sup>th</sup> house from AK is Cp and Saturn is Maheswara. Now let us say that Saturn is exalted in Li. From Saturn (Li), Venus owns the 8<sup>th</sup> house (Ta) and Mercury owns the 12<sup>th</sup> house (Vi). The stronger of Mercury and Venus becomes Maheswara. 

- (2) If Rahu or Ketu joins AK or the 8<sup>th</sup> from him, then we find the 6<sup>th</sup> lord from AK instead of the 8<sup>th</sup> lord. Please note that this is equivalent to taking the 8<sup>th</sup> lord in the anti-zodiacal order. Suppose AK is Mars and he is in Taurus. Then Sg is the 8<sup>th</sup> house from Mars and Jupiter, lord of Sg, becomes Maheswara. Now, suppose Ketu is in Ta or Sg. Because of Ketu’s presence, we find the 6<sup>th</sup> house from Mars instead of the 8<sup>th</sup> house. It is Li. Venus owns it and he becomes Maheswara.
