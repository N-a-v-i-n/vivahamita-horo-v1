###### **3.4.2** **_Temporary Relationships_** 

In addition to the permanent relationship, we have temporary relationships based on the planetary position in a chart. These temporary ( _tatkaala_ ) relationships are specific to a chart. 

Planets occupying the 2<sup>nd</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> , 11<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> rasis counted from the rasi occupied by a planet are its temporary friends. Planet occupying other rasis are its temporary enemies. 

**Example 4:** Let us consider Lord Sree Rama’s chart given in Figure 1 and find the temporary friends and temporary enemies of Sun and Moon. 

_Sun_ : Sun is in Ar. The 2<sup>nd</sup> , 3<sup>rd</sup> , 4<sup>th</sup> , 10<sup>th</sup> , 11<sup>th</sup> and 12<sup>th</sup> rasis counted from Ar are Ta, Ge, Cn, Cp, Aq and Pi. Planets in those rasis are Mercury, Moon, Jupiter, Mars and Venus. They are temporary friends of Sun in this chart. Saturn is the only temporary enemy.
