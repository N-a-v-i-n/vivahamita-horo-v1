## **36. Muhurta or Electional Astrology** 

#### **36.1 Introduction** 

When a person is born, the planetary position at that time decides how his/her life progresses. Similarly, when a new task is freshly started, the planetary position at that time decides how the task progresses. For example, the planetary position  at the time one joins in a new job decides how one’s new job goes. The planetary position at the time one starts a new company decides the fortune of the company. The planetary position at the time of a wedding decides how the marriage goes.
