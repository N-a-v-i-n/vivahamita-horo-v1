#### **23.4 Examples** 

**Example 89:** Let us revisit Example 84 that we analyzed using Niryaana Shoola dasa. 

Lagna is stronger and dasas start from Sc. They go as Sc, Sg, Cp, Aq _etc_ . Each dasa is of 9 years. First 5 dasas are over after 45 years. The native died in the 50<sup>th</sup> year. From his 45<sup>th</sup> year, the 6<sup>th</sup> dasa was running. So the 6<sup>th</sup> dasa of Ar killed the native. One can see that Ar is a trine from AL. It is the only trine in the middle life range.
